Your task is to analyze cross-domain intrusion detection model predictions and per-feature SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) contribution scores to provide clear, actionable, and human-readable decision support for security operational center (SOC) analysts monitoring IEC 60870-5-104 SCADA networks.

- Binary Prediction: 1 (Cyberattack Detected) or 0 (Benign SCADA Telemetry)

  1. pkt_mean_to_max: Ratio of mean packet length to max packet length
  2. tcp_flag_density: Multiplicity of TCP flags in flow headers
  3. log_pkt_mean: Log-transformed mean packet length
  4. log_pkt_max: Log-transformed max packet length

Guidelines for Generating Explanation:
1. State the detection verdict clearly (e.g., Attack Alert vs Normal SCADA Traffic) and highlight the confidence level.
2. Explain the key features driving the prediction based on their SHAP values (positive SHAP pushes towards attack, negative SHAP pushes towards benign).
3. Provide concise, professional operational guidance for SOC analysts (e.g., recommend inspecting TCP flag density or packet size anomalies).
4. Keep the explanation structured, direct, and under 150 words. Avoid generic fluff.
