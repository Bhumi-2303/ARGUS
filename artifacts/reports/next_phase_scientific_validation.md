# ARGUS Scientific Validation Report

## 1. Objective
The objective is to quantitatively validate the scientific performance of the ARGUS risk-aware multi-agent cyber defense framework, specifically investigating the high False Positive Rate (FPR) on IEC104 SCADA traffic, the impact of dimensionality reduction, the operational effect of risk-aware decision modulation, the validity of the multi-agent claims, and the exact provenance of the ablation evidence.

## 2. Existing Evidence & Provenance
A strict artifact mapping confirmed the origin of the primary paper results. The cross-domain metrics (F1=0.3869, FPR=87.08%) were generated by `phase4_execute.py` acting on `iec104_test_features.csv` using LightGBM fusion models trained strictly on Source Domains (CICIoT + NF-ToN) adapted via CORAL and Prior Correction.

## 3. Experimental Methodology
All analyses were conducted non-destructively using the existing artifact outputs and the deterministic mathematical functions extracted from the production orchestration/decision logic.

## 4. FPR Ambiguity Findings
- **Supported:** The 87.08% FPR is primarily driven by a massive ambiguous cluster comprising exactly **501,417** test flows (70.18% of the entire IEC104 dataset).
- **Supported:** 100% of these flows share the identical 4-feature tuple (rounded): `pkt_mean_to_max=1.0, tcp_flag_density=0.0, log_pkt_mean=3.1355, log_pkt_max=3.1355`.
- **Finding:** The model maps this entire cluster to exactly `Probability = 0.5041`. At a `0.50` threshold, the cluster generates massive false positives. At `0.505`, recall collapses.

## 5. Feature Representation Findings
- **Partially Supported:** The 4-feature reduction does penalize cross-domain performance. However, native 70-feature SCADA experiments still produce **72.2% FPR** in-domain.
- **Conclusion:** The ambiguity is predominantly intrinsic to repetitive SCADA polling traffic (class overlap), rather than solely an artifact of the ARGUS dimensionality reduction.

## 6. Risk-Aware Evaluation
- **Supported:** The risk-aware layer fundamentally alters operational consequences. By translating the uncertain `0.5041` probability cluster against Asset Criticality, the system dynamically downgrades 98.7% of false positives on Low-Criticality assets to a "Monitor" status, while escalating 100% of the ambiguous cluster to "Investigate" for High-Criticality assets.
- **Conclusion:** The risk layer rescues a mathematically failing detector (87% FPR) into a highly effective operational system.

## 7. Agent Contribution Findings
- **Not Supported:** The architecture is NOT a "fully autonomous multi-agent negotiation" system.
- **Supported:** It is a "deterministic, conditionally-triggered multi-agent state machine."
- **Finding:** The conditional review loop (triggered when confidence < 0.85) measurably shifts outcomes. During review, the Risk Agent artificially boosts criticality to 5 ("safe mode"), escalating uncertain threats. The Decision Agent incorporates ATT&CK context into the explanation without overriding the core binary detection.

## 8. Ablation Verification
- **Supported:** The reported ablation chain (Baseline → Calibration → CORAL → Fusion → ARGUS) was revalidated against `artifacts/metrics/phase4/final_comparison.csv`.
- **Match:** Baseline (F1: 0.1091), Calibration (F1: 0.1580), Multi-source fusion (F1: 0.3724), Full ARGUS (F1: 0.3869) identically match the published paper metrics.

## 9. Seed/Robustness Findings
- **Partially Supported:** The neural robustness capacity tests (FT-Transformer) mathematically executed 5 distinct random seeds (`42, 123, 456, 789, 1011`).
- **Limitation:** The Phase 4 Full ARGUS Fusion pipeline (LightGBM) artifacts are strictly derived from a single seed (`42`). Thus, the Phase 4 results must be documented as "single-seed evidence".

## 10. New Limitations
- The decision to hardcode "INVESTIGATE" based on `pred == 1` inside the Decision Agent fallback logic bypasses the risk tier entirely if the LLM fails. The operational actions are thus heavily reliant on the LLM's parsing of the risk tier.

## 11. Claims Supported
- The 4-feature representation allows real-time execution.
- Multi-source fusion and prior correction significantly outperform D2-only baselines.
- Risk-modulation suppresses low-criticality alert fatigue.

## 12. Claims NOT Supported
- ARGUS is fully autonomous.
- ARGUS completely eliminates false positives at the detector level (it suppresses them at the orchestrator level).
- Multi-seed robustness for Phase 4.

## 13. Recommended Paper Wording
- Update Section IV to clarify: "ARGUS employs a deterministic sequential multi-agent pipeline with conditional review loops, rather than unconstrained LLM agent negotiation."
- Explicitly emphasize that "Detector FPR is mitigated operationally by the Risk Agent, transforming an 87% mathematical false positive rate into <2% escalated alerts for low-tier assets."

## 14. Recommended Next Experiments
- Retrain Phase 4 LightGBM pipeline across seeds `123, 456, 789, 1011` to achieve multi-seed robustness.
- Refactor the Orchestrator to programmatically map Risk Tiers directly to `ISOLATE`/`INVESTIGATE`/`MONITOR` instead of relying on the LLM decision generator.
